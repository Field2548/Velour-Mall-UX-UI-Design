## The Problem

Mall visitors often encounter several frustrations that detract from their shopping experience:
*   **Getting Lost:** Complex mall layouts make finding specific stores difficult and time-consuming.
*   **Decision Fatigue:** Being overwhelmed by the sheer number of stores and restaurants, making it hard to choose where to go.
*   **Parking Hassles:** Wasting time searching for an available parking spot and later struggling to remember where the car was parked.
*   **Wasted Time:** Physically waiting in long queues for popular restaurants.

---

## The Solution

The **Velour Mall app** is a digital companion designed to be an all-in-one solution for a seamless, personalized, and stress-free shopping journey. The app empowers users with tools for navigation, discovery, and planning, turning a potentially frustrating trip into an enjoyable experience.

---

## Key Features

### 1. Main Feed (Home Screen)
The central hub for discovery and easy access to all features.
-   **Universal Search Bar:** Quickly find stores, products, or restaurants.
-   **Store Categories:** Browse curated categories like "Restaurant," "Dessert," and "Fashion."
-   **Coupons & Promotions:** A dedicated section for current deals and offers.
-   **Recommended Stores:** A personalized feed of stores based on user preferences and trending data.
-   **Quick-Access Navigation:** A bottom navigation bar for instant access to the AI Chat, Map, Parking, and Profile.

### 2. Intelligent Search
A powerful search system to help users find exactly what they need.
-   **Recent & Trending Searches:** See popular queries and access your search history.
-   **Trending Stores:** Discover the most popular stores and restaurants in the mall in real-time.

### 3. Detailed Store Pages
A comprehensive, all-in-one view for every store and restaurant.
-   **Core Information:** Name, price range, and descriptive tags (e.g., "Sushi," "Japanese").
-   **Queue & Reservation:** View live wait times and book a spot in a virtual queue online.
-   **Promotions & Menu:** Access exclusive in-app promotions and view full restaurant menus.
-   **User Reviews:** Read ratings and reviews from other shoppers.
-   **Instant Navigation:** A "View map" button to locate the store and start navigation immediately.

### 4. Indoor Navigation (Map)
An advanced indoor mapping system to ensure users never get lost.
-   **Standard View:** Shows the user's real-time location on a detailed mall map.
-   **Navigation Mode:** Provides turn-by-turn directions, estimated walking time, and clear instructions for multi-floor routes (e.g., "Take the escalator to Floor 2").

### 5. Smart Parking Assistance
A complete parking solution from arrival to departure.
-   **Pre-Parking:** View a real-time map of all parking levels, showing the number of available spots in each zone.
-   **Post-Parking:**
    -   **Car Marker:** Save your exact parking spot with a single tap.
    -   **Parking Timer:** A countdown shows when complimentary parking expires, with information on how to extend it.
    -   **Navigate to Car:** Get step-by-step directions from anywhere in the mall back to your parked vehicle.

### 6. AI Chatbot Assistant
A virtual assistant for personalized guidance and information.
-   **Guided Queries:** Suggestion buttons for common questions (Stores, Restaurants, etc.) and a keyboard for custom queries.
-   **Personalized Recommendations:** Ask for recommendations like "a quiet place for coffee" and receive tailored suggestions.
-   **Seamless Integration:** The chatbot can provide information, link directly to store pages, or initiate navigation.

### 7. User Profile & Gamification
A personalized space that encourages engagement.
-   **Membership System:** View loyalty points and member tier (e.g., "Platinum Member").
-   **Profile Management:** Edit personal details and manage app settings.
-   **Gamification:** The points and ranking system were implemented based on user feedback to increase engagement.

## Design Process & Iteration

This project followed an iterative design process based on user feedback. Initial low-fidelity and high-fidelity prototypes were created and then refined to address usability issues.

**Key examples of feedback-driven improvements:**
*   **Feedback:** "The store pages feel empty and lack useful information."
    *   **Solution:** Redesigned the page to include promotions, user reviews, menus, and detailed descriptions.
*   **Feedback:** "The chatbot is too text-heavy and requires typing for everything."
    *   **Solution:** Introduced interactive buttons for common queries (Stores, Restaurants, Mall) to reduce typing and streamline the user flow.
*   **Feedback:** "It's unclear how many complimentary parking hours are left."
    *   **Solution:** Added a parking timer and a "Find your car" button that initiates navigation back to the vehicle.

## Visual Identity

The visual design is guided by a **"Luxurious & Classic"** mood board. The color palette, typography, and imagery were chosen to create a sophisticated, clean, and high-end feel that aligns with the Velour Mall brand.
